## 4. Unresolved or not verified

1. **POSH Act, 2013 and Rules**: not re-read against official text. A search for 2025 or 2026 amendments or new Supreme Court directions failed (search tool error) and was not repeated.
2. **Supreme Court larger-bench review of the "industry" definition** (Bangalore Water Supply): current status not confirmed.
3. **Statutory floor wage**: the central rules state no amount; whether a rate has since been notified is not confirmed.
4. **Aggregator contribution rate** for gig workers: Ministry FAQ of 25 Oct 2026 says it "will be notified".
5. **Aggregator upload deadline**: 45 days from 8 May 2026 is 22 June 2026 if counting starts the next day; the source says 21 June 2026.
6. **State rules**: status and content for each state not tabulated. Central thresholds in the pages apply to the central sphere; state rules govern most private establishments.
7. **Case facts** (Maruti Suzuki, Honda, Wistron, Samsung, Tata Steel), **state gig-worker laws**, **ILO ratification status**, **penalty amounts under the Code on Wages**, maternity and employee-compensation figures, the migrant-worker wage ceiling, SAIL (2001) and the six laws in 11.7 (including DPDP Rules phase-in): taken from the source, not independently re-verified.
8. Retrenchment worked example uses a 26-day divisor; section 70 says "fifteen days' average pay" without a divisor.
9. Rule 49(4) of the Social Security Rules mentions sixty years for gig-worker benefits; not read in full.
10. Rule 98(8) of the OSH Rules (principal employer pays on contractor default) was read as referring to 15 days; exact wording not confirmed.
