# Engagement chapter: sources and evidence limits

Checked 16 November 2026. This is a targeted verification record for Chapter 5, not a comprehensive review of employment law or health guidance.

The full eleven-section HR Knowledge Book chapter supplies the chapter order, explanations, tables, profile connections and five interview answers. Matching PlacementOS revision notes, updated 26 October 2026, add the Gallup definition distinction, report/data-year distinction and a practical interview-answer heuristic. Private source documents and project records are not included in this repository.

## Matters left qualified

- Karnataka menstrual-leave eligibility, establishment coverage, implementation and current court position were not established from a retrieved primary policy document. The original source statement remains in the full reading block and is clearly flagged before quoting.
- Overseas disconnect laws, state-specific working hours, medical-check-up applicability and every legal exception were not comprehensively rechecked. The page's scoped evidence panel identifies these limits.
- Aon's India attrition figures and Tata Steel's historical dates remain dated source material. This update does not establish current benchmarks or independently verify every historical welfare milestone.
- Claims about best-employer rankings are source examples, not a current ranking or proof of employee experience.
- IBM's attrition-prediction use and Amber by inFeedo are reported examples in the book, not an independent evaluation of either product.
- The unreadable IndiGo quotation mentioned in PlacementOS was excluded as a factual company case.
- Worker classification depends on the actual relationship and applicable law. The workforce explorer's categories do not decide legal status.

## Illustrations and calculations

The chapter's scenarios, personas, process scenes, JD-R pathways and driver quadrant are conceptual. No company survey scores, clinical assessments, causal estimates or project outcomes are invented. Company marks are simplified inline typographic cues.

eNPS uses all responses in the denominator, including passives, and permits scores from -100 to +100. Annual attrition uses separations divided by average headcount; starting-cohort retention uses the original cohort. Monthly annualisation is an approximation and needs multiplication by 100 when expressed as a percentage. Confidentiality minimums illustrate a chosen reporting policy and do not guarantee anonymity.
